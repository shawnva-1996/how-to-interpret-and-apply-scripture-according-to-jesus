Pag sa sobrang religious person or counselor ka humingi ng payo, madalas lugi ka. Akala mo lang panalo ka, pero obserbahan mong mabuti, buksan mo ang iyong mata at tenga, at malalaman mong ikaw si Luigi. Lugi ka, talo, dejado. Bakit? Usually, it is because of an inappropriate choice of verses. May ire-refer sa iyo ang counselor or teacher na verses pero hindi naman laging tama ang kanyang napipiling verses. Lugi ka because of misapplication. Let us say, tama ang verse na nahugot para i-apply sa buhay mo pero kung minsan mali ang pag-apply. At malaki ang tsansa na maging mali ang pag-apply ng mga verses. Ang New Testament ay mahigit dalawang libong taon na at ang mga verses ng Old Testament ay mahigit tatlong libong taon na. At higit pang matanda dito yung oral traditions na pinagkunan ng written format of the Old Testament. Mula ito sa malayong lugar at panahon, kakaibang tao na may ibang ugali at kaugalian, ibang kultura ng mga nagsalita, nagturo at nagpairal ng mga katuruang ito.

> If you ask advice from an overly religious person or counselor, you usually lose out. You only think you are winning, but if you observe carefully, open your eyes and ears, you will realize you are "Luigi" — you lose, you are defeated, you are at a disadvantage. Why? Usually, it is because of an inappropriate choice of verses. The counselor or teacher may refer you to verses, but the verses he chooses are not always right. You lose because of misapplication. Let us say, the verse drawn out to apply to your life is correct, but sometimes the application is wrong. And there is a big chance that the application of the verses will be wrong. The New Testament is more than two thousand years old, and the verses of the Old Testament are more than three thousand years old. And even older than these are the oral traditions from which the written format of the Old Testament came. These came from a distant place and time, different people with different habits and customs, a different culture of those who spoke, taught, and enforced these teachings.

Paano mo huhugutin sa kanilang lugar at panahon ang mga verses na ito at ia-apply nang tama sa iyong lugar at panahon ngayon? Madalas magka-bisala. Madalas malulugi ka because of out-of-context application of verses.

> How will you draw these verses out of their place and time and apply them correctly in your place and time today? Often you will get it backwards. You will often lose out because of out-of-context application of verses.

This kind of counselors will even make you feel guilty for being human.

Sa Greek culture which was prevalent during New Testament times, mina-masama nila ang lahat ng bagay na may kinalaman sa mundo, sa lupa at sa katawan. Ang minamabuti lang nila ay yung may kinalaman sa espiritu, sa espirituwal at sa kabilang buhay.
 Pero magkakasama sa isang tao ang katawan, espiritu at kaluluwa. In Greek culture, which was prevalent during New Testament times, they regarded as bad everything that has to do with the world, the earth, and the body. They regarded as good only what has to do with the spirit, the spiritual, and the life to come.
> But in a person, body, spirit, and soul go together. The fashionable kind of faith and of reading and applying God's will to human life in New Testament times was that the body is bad, fight your appetites, all material things are evil. Jesus did not teach that, but that was the culture of the New Testament churches because they were mostly in Greek culture-influenced environments. That is why it is important for you to separate and sift out which is the true teaching of Jesus and which is the culture of the towns his teachings reached.

Mahalaga ring malaman kung yung pagtuturo nya ay naihalo na ng mga bayan-bayan na yon sa mga dati na nilang kaugalian.
Hindi galing kay Hesus yung sobrang pagtanggi sa ginhawa, sarap at mga gusto ng katawan. > The extreme rejection of comfort, pleasure, and the desires of the body did not come from Jesus. His very first miracle was even to make wine from water so that the joy at a wedding party would continue. He was always at gatherings, so the priests labeled him greedy, a drunkard, a drinker, voracious, and a glutton. Because truly, whenever there was a meal, he ate.

> Whenever there were feasts, he went, rejoiced, and mingled with people. Which Jesus do we really know? The true Jesus of the Gospels? Or the Jesus of the church?

If you are told again and again to control yourself, endure, do not do what you want, be patient, do not speak up — there is like a volcano inside you wanting to erupt, on whom will you now vent it? On your future students if you are a Bible study teacher, on your future flock if you are a preacher, on your future members if you are a pastor. Na bago nya tinanggap ay pinag-aralan muna ang puno at dulo, ang mga relasyon ng mga verses sa ibang mga verses, ang pagkakaiba ng mga verses sa, at least, three versions or translations of the Bible para talagang hanapin yung pinakagitnang kahulugan? Gaano karami rin yung pag may sinabi yung preacher o teacher ay magsasaliksik kung totoo at tama nga ba ang sinasabi nila? Wala. Kadalasan, yung mga matatapang na naghahanap ng mga giyera, debate at pakikipagpaligsahan sa mga ibang mananampalataya ang pinanghahawakan ay yung nadinig lang nila. Hindi naman nila sinaliksik pero buung-buo nilang nilulon at pinaniwalaan. At ngayon, papatay o magpapakamatay sila para sa ganung pananalig.

> Because that is really their belief and conviction. At least, they are honest and sincere. But they can be sincerely wrong. How many, too, when a preacher or teacher says something, will research whether what they say is really true and correct? None. Usually, the bold ones who seek wars, debates, and competition with other believers hold on only to what they heard. They did not research it, but they swallowed it whole and believed it. Una, dahil ang Biblia ay libro, dapat marunong kang magbasa. Hindi ka lang dapat marunong magbasa ng abakada, dapat marunong ka ring umunawa ng wika kasi ang Biblia ay nasa iba't-ibang wika. > First, because the Bible is a book, you must know how to read. You must not only know how to read the alphabet, you must also know how to understand language, because the Bible is in different languages. There is English, Tagalog, Cebuano, while these were merely translated from Greek and Hebrew. In translation alone, so much is already lost.

'Tapos ang dami-daming versions na pino-produce ng mga kongregasyon na may kani-kanyang doktrina. Dahil ang gusto nila ay yung doktrina nila ang nakalagay sa version na yon, yun lang ang version na ipinagpipilitan nila sa takot na ma-expose ang kanilang tagapakinig sa ibang mga stand o pananaw regarding the same verses.

> Then there are so many versions produced by congregations, each with its own doctrine. Because they want their doctrine to be in that version, that is the only version they insist on, for fear that their listeners will be exposed to other stands or views regarding the same verses.

They control the version that you will read para hindi ka ma-expose sa ibang ideya. Natatakot sila na matuto ka e.

> They control the version that you will read so that you will not be exposed to other ideas. They are afraid that you might learn.

> They control the interpretation and the application, and in the end, only their church is right and everyone else is wrong.

Meron ba talagang isang church na 100% tama at ang ibang churches ay 99% wrong? Isang kagila-gilalas na kasinungalingan yan na pinaiiral mula sa pulpito at mga Bible schools para paglingkuran nila, itaguyod at itanyag ang sarili nilang kongregasyon, hindi ang tunay at bukas na isip na pag-aaral.

> Is there really one church that is 100% right and the other churches 99% wrong? That is an astonishing lie promoted from the pulpit and Bible schools so that they can serve, promote, and publicize their own congregation, not true and open-minded study.

Binubulag at pinaliliit ang paningin ng mga miembro para wala nang ibang malaman pa. Pinagbabawalang makinig kay ganito at pinagbabawalang umattend sa ganun dahil sinusupil at kino-control ang utak.

> Members are blinded and their vision narrowed so that they will know nothing else. They are forbidden to listen to this person and forbidden to attend that, because the mind is suppressed and controlled.
